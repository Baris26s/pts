Detailed description

PTS Defense provides OEM production for the Groza-1/2 electronic warfare systems, aligning quality assurance with NATO and EU standards. We manage configuration baselines, serial tracking, and documentation to support fleet readiness.

Our teams coordinate qualification testing, export compliance, and knowledge transfer to ensure rapid ramp-up and sustainment for partner nations.
