Detailed description

Falcon combines compact LRUs with high-speed digital receivers to detect, classify, and jam emerging threats. Its SDR core enables mission-driven profiles with low latency reaction.

The system supports modular antennas for omnidirectional or directional patterns, ensuring coverage from low-altitude helicopter operations to fixed-wing standoff missions.
