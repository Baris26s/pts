Detailed description

RF-45 can be reconfigured per mission through modular RF amplifier blocks. Its high dynamic range front-end preserves friendly signals while effectively denying hostile transmissions.

The embedded threat library and remote management interfaces allow field teams to update dynamic frequency plans within minutes. A software-defined architecture enables rapid addition of new modulations and waveforms.
