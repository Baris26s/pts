Detailed description

R-400 Sentinel combines compact form factor with long-range detection through optimized waveforms and low sidelobe antennas. Fast refresh rates support accurate track files even in cluttered environments.

The radar interfaces with command-and-control nodes via standard protocols, enabling rapid cueing of EO/IR, RF effectors, or kinetic interceptors within integrated air and missile defense architectures.
