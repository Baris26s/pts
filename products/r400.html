System Description

The PTS R-400 Sentinel is a compact, next-generation ground surveillance radar designed for detecting UAVs, ground vehicles and slow-moving threats with high precision.

Its solid-state architecture ensures high reliability, low maintenance and long-term operation in harsh battlefield environments. With full 360° azimuth scanning and advanced signal processing, R-400 delivers consistent detection performance day and night.
