System Description

Falcon is engineered for reconnaissance teams requiring low power consumption, silent operation modes and high mobility. Modular antenna options allow fast switching between directional jamming and full 360° coverage.

The integrated GNSS spoofing module disrupts hostile navigation systems while preserving friendly GNSS services through selective filtering. Mission profiles can be updated via software for evolving operational requirements.
